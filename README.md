# Chaty Live: practice chat for new streamers

**Never stream to an empty chat again.** Chaty Live is a Windows app that shows a simulated **practice chat** that reacts to what you say and what you play, so you can practice talking to chat before real viewers show up. It works in the app and as a see-through **OBS overlay**.

**[Download Chaty Live for Windows](https://github.com/dovis00/chaty-live/releases/latest/download/Chaty-Live-Setup.exe)** · [Website](https://chaty-live.whop.site) · [Setup guide](https://chaty-live.whop.site/setup.html) · [Terms & Privacy](https://chaty-live.whop.site/legal.html)

## What it does
- Hears your voice and sees your game, then chat reacts to it, like a flight simulator for streaming.
- Shows up in OBS as a transparent browser-source overlay, always labelled **PRACTICE CHAT**.
- Runs entirely on your PC: speech-to-text and the chat AI are local. No AI fees, no tracking.

## What it never does
- Never posts to Twitch, YouTube, Kick, TikTok or any other platform.
- Never creates fake viewers, follows or views, and never shows fake sub, follow or donation alerts.
